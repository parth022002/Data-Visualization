# Key Insights and Strategic Recommendations — Regional Retail

## Summary of Empirical Findings
1. East region drives maximum overall sales (₹7,80,759), while South delivers the highest transaction ticket size (₹1,089.63).
2. October marks the highest revenue (₹2,88,562) and profit (₹63,768) peak of the annual cycle.
3. Home Décor and Electronics are the primary margin drivers, contributing disproportionately to store-level EBITDA.
