# Project Overview — Adani Green Energy Transition

## Strategic Context
India has committed to reaching 500 GW of non-fossil fuel energy capacity by 2030 as part of its COP26 climate commitments. Adani Green Energy Limited (AGEL) plays a pivotal role in this transition.

## Analytical Approach
1. **Landscape Benchmarking:** Cross-comparing potential across Indian states to demonstrate that clean energy potential vastly exceeds remaining thermal capacity.
2. **Capacity Deconstruction:** Modeling technology shifts from standalone solar towards co-located hybrid assets.
3. **Financial Translation:** Tracking the conversion of capital deployment into long-term tariff revenues.
