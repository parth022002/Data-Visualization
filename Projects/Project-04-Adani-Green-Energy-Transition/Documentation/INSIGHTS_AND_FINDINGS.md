# Key Insights and Strategic Findings — AGEL Renewable Story

## Key Observations
1. **The Energy Turning Point:** Renewable potential across top states (Rajasthan, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka) dwarfs non-renewable alternatives by more than 10:1, confirming India's long-term clean energy cost advantage.
2. **Khavda as a Global Benchmark:** AGEL's Khavda Renewable Park represents the single largest hybrid installation worldwide, capable of generating clean power for over 16 million homes.
3. **PPA Revenue Protection:** With ~90% of operational capacity tied to sovereign-backed 25-year PPAs at fixed tariffs, AGEL maintains high revenue predictability that insulates cash flows from merchant market power price volatility.
